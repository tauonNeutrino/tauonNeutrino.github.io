I am working to bring quantum information science and techniques into my future, hopefully as a career. Continual advances in quantum simulation, across many architectures and regimes, have enamored me into studying how I can bring value to this field. My current developments are focused around < 1 > quantum circuit simulation with Qiskit, < 2 > the foundations of tensor networks and alternative descriptions of quantum systems, and < 3 > the basics in chemical models. 

One year after graduating, I have a more clear outlook. My goal is to become a quantum software/theoretical engineer and co-develop simulation models, theory and applications for quantum chemistry, security and information science applications! 

Please contact me with any research opportunities or questions regarding research opportunities or inquiries. I will always be full time employed - it may take a day or two for me to respond.
