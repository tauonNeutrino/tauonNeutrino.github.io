An excellent first foray into the math and concepts behind elementary Quantum Computing. This alleviated the majority of my questions, and the practice problems are the most understandable by me from any QC textbook I have read. While the writing style is sometimes too pedantic and repetitive, the redundancy of definitions means that any reader is more likely to understand the concepts! 

I highly recommend this to beginning QC engineers, and I think that I will keep my softcover version until it erodes away to time.
