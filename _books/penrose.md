This is hands down the best physics book I have read, and I strongly believe that it belongs in modern physics classes as at least a supplemental source. The drawings are incredibly illustrative, and the content that Penrose thoroughly explains covers a strong backbone (nearly complete) of mathematical foreknowledge. This is necessary, because he gives excellent perspectives on pretty advanced stuff in the back.

I think there is always something to learn from this book, and I aspire to reach a level of teacher that Penrose is. I really recommend it!
