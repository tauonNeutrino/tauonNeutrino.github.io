I am taking up a rather exhausting full time job, which will complicate my time along with the magic group and grad school applications. Besides that, I am taking an interest in quantum entanglement, specifically how it spreads through operator scrambling and the Lieb-Robinson bound. 

I personally think many of the big mysteries of QM and its related topics stem from our lack of understanding on how wavefunctions are separated between different particles, overlap and interact, and how decoherence acts at a most basic level. I am looking into experiments that allow for more viewpoints into this matter - give me any ideas if you have any!
